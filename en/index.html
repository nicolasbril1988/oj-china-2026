Documents and entry to China

Argentine citizens can enter China visa free for stays of up to 30 days for tourism or business.

  • Passport: travel on an ordinary Argentine passport. We recommend at least six months of validity and a digital copy on your phone.
  • Arrival card: since November 2025 it can be completed online before the trip, on the National Immigration Administration website, in the NIA 12367 app or in the WeChat and Alipay mini programs. If you do not complete it beforehand, you can do it at the airport with a QR code, at the self service kiosks or on paper.
  • ID at destination: always carry your passport. It is requested at hotels, stations and on trains.
Connectivity and apps

In China, services such as Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok and Google do not work on the local network. The travel eSIM (see above) solves most of this. We still recommend installing a VPN before leaving as a backup: several of these apps cannot be downloaded once in China.

Payments

Alipay is the most practical way to pay: you can link a foreign Visa or Mastercard without a Chinese bank account, and it is accepted almost everywhere, including street stalls and the metro. Credit cards and cash are of limited use outside shopping malls.

  • Link your card and verify your identity before leaving, and tell your bank you will use the card in China to avoid blocks.
  • Bring a second card as a backup and a small amount of cash in yuan: some small shops do not accept foreign cards.
Weather and dress code

October is one of the most pleasant months in Shanghai: highs average 23 °C and lows 16 °C, with rain on about a third of the days. It gets cooler towards the end of the month, so dress in layers and bring an umbrella or a light rain jacket.

Meals, health and assistance
  • All meals in China are covered by Omoda & Jaecoo.
  • Travel assistance is provided by Omoda & Jaecoo.
  • Chinese cooking often uses nuts and chili. If you have allergies, intolerances or dietary restrictions, tell us before the trip so we can plan each meal.
  • Drink bottled water: tap water is not safe to drink.
Electricity

China uses 220 V and 50 Hz, the same as Argentina, so your chargers work without a converter. Sockets come in several formats: bring a universal adapter.

Local rules and customs
  • Cities have security cameras almost everywhere and rules are strictly enforced. Hotel items such as towels or bathrobes must not be taken from the room.
  • Tipping is not customary in China.
  • Travelling with an open mind and a willingness to try new things is the best way to enjoy the experience.
